# Economics of Education Review Submission Requirements Checked

Checked on 2026-05-29 from the official Elsevier/ScienceDirect Guide for Authors.

- Target article type: `Replication Studies`.
- EER encourages studies that address internal or external validity of a previously published article.
- The original article does not need to have appeared in EER.
- Successful replication studies should characterize the original article's research question, methodology, and findings.
- EER uses single-anonymized review, so the manuscript can include author details unless the submission system asks otherwise.
- EER/Elsevier expects separate article highlights at submission; this working copy includes `eer_highlights.txt`.
- EER requires disclosure of generative AI or AI-assisted technology use in manuscript preparation.
- Supplementary data/code can be submitted with the article or linked through an appropriate repository, subject to the original data's redistribution terms.

Primary source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/economics-of-education-review/publish/guide-for-authors
