Highlights - 

\begin{itemize}
    \item Local road-maintenance tax cuts lower maintenance funds by 11\%.
    \item After fine-tuning a Vision Transformer (ViT) model on satellite images of roads, our AI-based metric shows a 23\% drop in road quality.
    \item Median house prices fall 9\% over the ten-year period after failed renewal.
    \item Urban areas and high-value homes are most affected.
    \item Larger tax cuts lead to proportionally larger declines in house prices.
\end{itemize}
